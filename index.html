About Me

I am a library professional with more than 20 years of experience across academic, engineering and medical libraries in the UAE and India. My work covers library management, cataloguing and classification, circulation, reference services, electronic resources and user support.

I currently work at the Mohammed Bin Rashid University of Medicine and Health Sciences (MBRU) in Dubai, where I look after the theses and dissertations in the MBRU Knowledge Repository, run information literacy sessions and library events, and contribute to library planning, budgets and KPIs.
